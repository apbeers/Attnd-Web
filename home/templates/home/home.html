Everything your attendees look for

One link or QR code. Maps, times, what’s on now, and a nudge when it matters.

Built by AI, checked by you

From your website, flyer or spreadsheet to a finished event

Attnd reads what you already have: stages, booths, vendors, set times. It finds photos of your places, picks colours from your brand, and keeps watching your site for changes you can accept with a tap.

The app

Scan and you’re in, even with no signal

On iPhone the QR code opens an instant App Clip: nothing to install. On Android it opens Google Play, and Attnd opens straight on the event once installed. Everything works offline once loaded, with walking times and directions between places.

Updates and urgent alerts

Send “Doors open” or a weather change to every phone. Urgent ones break through, even on the lock screen.
